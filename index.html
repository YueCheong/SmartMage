SmartMage: Dynamic Modality Orchestration for 3D Scene Understanding

Yue Zhang, Yingzhao Jian, Yunqiu Xu, Xiaoxiao Sun, Hehe Fan
Zhejiang University & Stanford University

arXiv DOI Code Coming Soon

Abstract

SmartMage is a unified multimodal large language model for 3D scene understanding that dynamically orchestrates heterogeneous modalities through semantic-guided modality selection and modality-aware expert specialization.

Method

SmartMage introduces SMART for adaptive modality selection and MAGE for modality-aware expert routing.

Results

SmartMage achieves strong performance across five 3D scene understanding benchmarks.
